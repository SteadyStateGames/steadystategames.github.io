The thinking behind the tools.

01 / Test creation & digital systems

One assessment.
More than one use.

I designed Google Forms assessments for proctored computerized testing using the school’s available technology. Objective questions could be scored automatically, while written responses stayed organized for review.

The same workflow became study-guide practice, reducing grading time and giving students another way to prepare.

Google Forms · Chromebooks · Assessment design

How the system fits together +

The constraint: work within the school’s available testing tools.

The design: combine automatically scored objective questions with short-answer and essay responses organized for review.

The reusable part: adapt the assessment workflow for practice rather than build a separate study-guide process.

02 / Laboratory safety & process improvement

From an unmaintained cabinet to a usable system.

I took responsibility for the chemical cabinet and laboratory safety, designed a QR-code usage logbook and inventory system, and reorganized chemical storage using Flinn guidance.

That work also included disposing of a legacy container of 18 M sulfuric acid following Flinn procedures and improving containment for another acid container.

Chemical inventory · QR-code logging · Safety

What I changed +

Records: create an inventory system and a QR-code logbook to streamline chemical usage records.

Organization: move chemicals into storage arrangements aligned with Flinn safety guidance.

Responsibility: address legacy chemical hazards rather than leave them for the next person.
