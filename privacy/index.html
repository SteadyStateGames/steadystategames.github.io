Website Privacy notice

This notice covers this privacy hub, the main Steady State Games landing page, and the Cat Solitaire/Purrsonal Space and Solitaire browser game pages on steadystategames.dev. It does not describe other teaching pages or separate website tools.

GitHub Pages hosts these pages and logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy policy.

Browser games may keep local saves and preferences in local storage or IndexedDB and cache game files using service workers. We do not add advertising or analytics trackers to these covered pages. You can clear saved progress and cached files using your browser's site-data controls.

The landing page links to external itch.io pages and optional Stripe donations. If you follow those links, their privacy policies apply: itch.io and Stripe.

If you email steadystategames@gmail.com, Gmail handles the email under Google's privacy policy. We use your address and message to handle your request, keep it only as long as necessary for that purpose or legal obligations, and accept deletion requests at the same address.

We will update this notice and its date if these practices change.
