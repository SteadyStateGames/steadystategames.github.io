Privacy Policy - Solitaire Collection

Effective October 7, 2026

Steady State Games LLC's Android game works offline. It does not collect or send personal information to us or third parties. It has no accounts, ads, in-app purchases, analytics, or remote crash-reporting service. This applies to players of all ages.

Settings, statistics, saved games, and undo history stay in the app's private storage. Android protects that storage; we do not add separate encryption or receive those files. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove local saves. Cloud backup is disabled, although Android device-to-device transfers may vary.

If you email steadystategames@gmail.com, we receive your address and message through Gmail. We use this information to handle your request and keep it only as long as necessary for that purpose or legal obligations. You can request deletion at the same address.

Google Play and Android may separately process store activity and diagnostics under their own policies. See Google's privacy policy. Our website's hosting practices are covered in the Website Privacy notice.

We'll update this page and its date if these practices change.
